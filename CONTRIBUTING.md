# Contributing

This is a proprietary repository owned by Upcraft Solutions Private Limited.
Contributions are accepted only from authorized contributors under the
company's applicable employment, contractor, confidentiality, and intellectual
property agreements.

Before contributing:

1. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and all three governing documents in `docs/`.
2. Keep changes aligned with the educational-video workflow, benchmark gates,
   and model-routing policy.
3. Obtain explicit product approval before changing a governing document,
   medical-safety policy, release gate, or approved model default.
4. Include appropriate tests, QA evidence, and documentation updates with each
   implementation change.
5. Do not commit credentials, customer data, private generated assets, or
   third-party material without documented rights and approval.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you are authorized to do so and
that all associated intellectual-property rights are assigned or licensed to
Upcraft Solutions Private Limited as required by your governing agreement.
